FAQ

Questions, answered

Which platforms are supported?

YouTube, Twitch, Kick and Rumble links are detected automatically, plus any direct HLS/m3u8 stream — useful for live TV channels. Just paste the URL.

How many streams can I add?

Up to 12 streams in a single grid. The layout scales from a single full-screen player up to a 4×3 wall.

Can I share my grid?

Yes. The Share button copies a link that encodes your exact set of streams. Anyone who opens it sees the same wall.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — on narrow screens the grid stacks vertically so every stream stays watchable.

Why won't a Twitch stream load?

Twitch embeds only play on real hosted domains (the player checks a parent parameter). Host this file on any domain — even a free static host — and Twitch tiles will work.
